Kitchen Shift & Service Flow

Each day can use different start/finish times. The key is a clear handover so breakfast, lunch, dinner and next-day preparation continue without gaps.
Opening → Breakfast → Lunch → Dinner → Closing
Opening shift · example 07:00Log in and complete opening checks. Review guest numbers, menu, dietary requirements and allergens. Set up and cook breakfast.
Breakfast productionBaked beans · hash browns · grilled tomato · pancakes · tofu · mushrooms · porridge. Maintain buffet/service, then clean down.
Lunch preparationAs breakfast service settles, check the lunch menu and production requirements. Prepare and cook lunch with allergen/dietary controls confirmed.
Before early shift finishesOnce lunch is ready, prepare as much dinner mise en place as possible. Record what is complete and what remains for the incoming shift.
Later shift · example 12:00Take the lunch/service handover, support service and clean-down, finish dinner production/service, then prepare priority items for the following morning/day before closing.

Restaurant Shift Flow

Restaurant rota follows the same handover principle, with staffing adjusted to guest numbers and service times.
OpeningRestaurant opening checks · breakfast room/buffet setup · dietary/allergen briefing.
Breakfast serviceWelcome guests · maintain buffet and tables · communicate replenishment with kitchen · clear and reset.
Lunch setup & serviceCheck numbers/menu/diets · set restaurant · service · clear, clean and reset.
Dinner preparationIncoming team receives handover · confirms layout, guest needs and service plan.
Dinner & closingService · clear-down · cleaning · next-service setup where practical · closing sign-off and handover.
