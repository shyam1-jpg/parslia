One operational picture

Built around the reality of a working kitchen.

From the first delivery of the morning to final close-down, Parslia is designed to bring the kitchen team's operational information together without forcing staff to jump between paper, spreadsheets, folders and messages.

01

Command Centre

See service readiness, production, outstanding actions, temperatures, cleaning, equipment and training from one operational view.

02

Menus & Recipes

Structure recipes, portions, yields, allergens and production quantities so the kitchen can move from menu to prep with less duplication.

03

Production

Organise prep and service work by dish, batch, team member and operational status, with a clear path from preparation to service.

04

Food Safety

Bring kitchen checks and food-safety records into a controlled digital workflow with corrective action and management review where configured.

05

Allergen Control

Keep allergen and dietary information visible at the points where kitchen decisions are made. Final controls should follow each site's approved food-safety procedures.

06

SOPs & Training

Combine written procedures, equipment imagery and training video into an accessible staff learning and operating library.

07

Cleaning

Organise daily, close-down and deep-clean work with clearer ownership, evidence and management visibility.

08

Equipment

Connect operating guidance, cleaning procedures, training
