How a subscriber builds

1. Start

Free includes 25 credits. Paid plans add more, and they can download the file. Every plan lasts 30 days on this browser.

2. Plan, then build

Plan shows the screens and the credit cost before anything changes. Approve builds it. Discuss answers for 1 credit and leaves the preview alone.

3. Publish

Super Pro names the public link and saves that version. Later edits stay in the studio until you publish again. Unpublish takes the link offline.

What an app includes

The same jobs the other builders advertise, done inside the file Kiln makes.

Records

Lists, forms, and a home count. Saved in the browser that opens the app.

Sign-in

Ask for members, login, or roles. The first account is the owner. Later accounts are staff.

Orders

A shop or a payments request adds an order list. Cards are not charged from the app.

Any subject
