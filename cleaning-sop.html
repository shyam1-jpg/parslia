Daily & Weekly Cleaning of Commercial Kitchen Equipment

Vedānta operating procedure for routine and weekly cleaning of the deep fryer, cooking line/range, extraction hood and filters, and walk-in chilled/frozen storage. This procedure links cleaning, COSHH controls, equipment faults, staff completion and supervisor verification.

Before cleaning

Use only approved Vedānta cleaning products and follow the product label, COSHH assessment and Safety Data Sheet for dilution, contact time, PPE, storage and emergency action.

STOP: Never mix cleaning chemicals. Never use an unlabelled chemical container. Do not begin equipment cleaning until the equipment is safely shut down, isolated where required and at a safe temperature.

CLN-01 · Deep Fryer

  1. 01
    Switch off and isolate
    Follow the fryer shutdown/isolation procedure before cleaning.
  2. 02
    Allow oil to cool
    Do not drain or clean hot oil. Follow the approved safe oil-draining procedure and equipment instructions.
  3. 03
    Drain/filter safely
    Use the approved waste-oil or filtration container. Never pour oil into a sink or drain.
  4. 04
    Clean accessible surfaces
    Remove grease and food debris from exterior surfaces, handles and surrounding splash areas using the approved product.
  5. 05
    Inspect
    Check for leaks, damaged controls, ignition problems or other faults. Report faults before returning to use.
Weekly: Carry out the manufacturer-approved deep clean/boil-out only using the approved chemical and equipment-specific procedure.

CLN-02 · Cooking Line / Range

  1. 01
    Switch off
    Allow hot surfaces to cool to a safe cleaning temperature.
  2. 02
    Remove debris
    Remove food and grease using the correct scraper/pad for the surface.
  3. 03
    Degrease
    Use the approved cleaning product according to its label and COSHH controls.
  4. 04
    Clean removable trays
    Wash grease trays/collection parts and refit only when clean and dry.
  5. 05
    Food-contact surfaces
    Clean and disinfect/sanitise as required by the approved product procedure and allow appropriate contact time.
  6. 06
    Report faults
    Any abnormal flame, damaged control, electrical fault or unsafe condition must be reported.

CLN-03 · Extraction Hood & Filters

  1. 01
    Keep extraction operating during cooking
    Do not operate relevant cooking equipment without required extraction.
  2. 02
    Clean accessible surfaces
    When safe, degrease the hood exterior, accessible surfaces and grease collection areas.
  3. 03
    Inspect filters
    Check for excessive grease loading or damage. Escalate if cleaning cannot restore a safe condition.
  4. 04
    Clean filters
    On the scheduled clean, remove filters safely and clean using the approved method, then dry and refit correctly.
Professional extract cleaning: Routine staff cleaning does not replace the specialist cleaning/inspection frequency set by the site's fire-risk assessment, insurer, contractor and applicable UK extract-system requirements. Retain contractor certificates.

CLN-04 · Walk-In Fridge / Freezer

  1. 01
    Record temperature
    Complete the relevant Parslia temperature record and act on any out-of-limit result.
  2. 02
    Remove spills
    Clean spillages promptly using the approved food-area cleaning method.
  3. 03
    Check food
    Confirm food is covered, labelled, date-coded and within shelf life. Follow FIFO/FEFO as applicable.
  4. 04
    Clean floor and shelving
    Work in sections to minimise door-open time and protect food from contamination.
  5. 05
    Inspect unit
    Check door seals, lighting, internal release/alarm where fitted, ice build-up and obvious defects.

Weekly Deep Clean

Complete on the designated cleaning day or more frequently where risk, usage or visible contamination requires it.
