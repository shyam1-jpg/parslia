Get help with sign-in, working forms and procedure corrections.

Staff access and workspace questions

Contact the workspace manager at shyam.prasad@thevedanta.org for account access, responsibilities, records or local procedure approval. Use your own account; do not use another person’s login.

For a forgotten password, open Staff sign-in and choose “Forgot password?”. If the portal says sign-in is not configured or access is refused, contact the manager rather than creating repeated accounts.

Report a website problem

Include the page address, device/browser, what you were trying to do and the error message. A screenshot can help, but remove names, confidential records and account details first. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.

Find a procedure or form

Use the search at the top of the kitchen dashboard. Try an equipment name, a procedure code such as KP-04, or a task such as cleaning. You can also open the kitchen forms directory.

Why does the dashboard say “Not connected”?

The dashboard is currently a directory and planning workspace. It does not read live operational records. Blank figures mean no verified information is loaded here, not that a check passed or failed.

Urgent safety concern? Follow the house reporting and emergency procedures and contact the duty manager immediately. This page and email contact are not an emergency response service.
