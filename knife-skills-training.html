Knife Skills – Safe and Correct Use

Learn, practise and demonstrate safe professional knife handling one controlled step at a time.

STEP 1 OF 10

Prepare your workspace

Start with a clean, stable, well-lit work area. Secure the chopping board and select the correct knife for the task.

Safe work starts before the first cut.
10%
Training progress

Training Objectives

Understand knife-related hazards and responsibilities
Prepare a stable and hygienic workspace
Use controlled grip and guiding-hand technique
Carry, clean and store knives safely
Complete supervised practical competency

Stop & Report

Stop work if a knife is damaged, the board/work area is unstable, or conditions are unsafe. Follow the approved site SOP and report defects.
