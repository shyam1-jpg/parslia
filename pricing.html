Subscription

Credits come with a plan.

Free starts with 25 credits. A simple app is about 15–20. Several screens are about 30–50. A change is 12, and a look change is 2. Paid plans can download the file. Super Pro is the client plan: 2,000 credits, a public link you name, and the app shows your name.

Demo checkout. Choosing a plan adds the subscription on this browser. No card is charged. To take real payments, paste Stripe Payment Links into js/payments.js. After a real payment, Stripe should return here as paid.html?plan=starter or paid.html?top=top-100.
